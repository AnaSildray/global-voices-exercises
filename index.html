Activity 3 • Pragmatic Interaction Speaking & Mediation

Activity 3 – Cultural Mediator Role-Play

Instructions

  1. Enter the digital simulation of an international team experiencing a communication clash.
  2. Analyze direct vs. indirect communication styles and cultural friction.
  3. Select polite pragmatic responses using modal verbs (*could*, *would*) to preserve team climate.
Pragmatic Tip: Softening Requests
❌ "Do this now!" (Imposing)
✔️ "Could you please...?" (Polite)
